Method

Comparable pace, not raw track speed

Each point is one constructor in one race. Its representative pace is the mean of each entered driver's median among their fastest 25% of recorded laps, reducing the influence of pit stops, traffic, and safety-car laps. The pace gap is then measured against the fastest constructor in that same race, so the visual compares relative car performance across circuits.
